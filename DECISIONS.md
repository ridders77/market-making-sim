### Calibration method
- 2026-11-26 — Touch estimator, ask side uses buyer-initiated prints only (is_buyer_maker = False) and bid side seller-initiated only. — Only an aggressive buy can fill a resting ask.
- 2026-11-26 — δ grid 0 … 8 USDT in 0.1 steps; δ is measured from the mid in force just before the window starts. Exponential fit over δ ∈ [0.1, 5.0] USDT; δ = 0 (a print at the best quote) is excluded from the fit and its rate reported separately. — λ(δ) jumps by ~15× between δ = 0 and δ = 0.1: on BTCUSDT someone at the touch is hit about twice per second, but a print one tick beyond the touch needs the price to move or a sweep. ln λ is close to linear from 0.1 to 8 USDT. Excluding the touch is equivalent to assuming we sit at the back of the queue at the best price, which is the honest assumption for a new market maker.
- 2026-11-26 — Main window Δt = 1 s (the smallest the 1-second mids allow); Δt = 2 s and 5 s are reported as a sensitivity check. Points with p = 1 or fewer than 30 hits are excluded from the fit (none are in range at Δt = 1 s).
- 2026-11-26 — σ main estimate = realised volatility of 1-second mid changes (zero-mean MLE); 10 s and 60 s versions are reported as a microstructure check. — Specified by the owner; the comparison shows whether 1 s sampling is biased.
- 2026-11-26 — Bootstrap CIs resample days (not windows). — Windows within a day share that day's volatility, so days are the independent units.
- 2026-11-26 — Pre-registered out-of-sample verdict: "good" if the calibration-day fit predicts test-day λ(δ) within a factor 1.5 on average over the fit range (mean |ln(obs/pred)| < 0.4), "usable with care" up to a factor 2 (< 0.7), otherwise "poor". — Criterion fixed before seeing test results.

Using σ_1s understates the 1-hour inventory risk by ~25% in variance. Kept σ_1s as specified and flagged here.

Longer windows mix the fill process with diffusion of the mid, and bursts of trades count once per window. The 1 s values are the most local, closest to the simulator's "intensity relative to the current mid", and are the ones used.

### Stage 2 simulation results (results/stage2/strategy_comparison.md)
- A–S vs naive with the same average spread, 1,000 paired paths, no fee: means are statistically indistinguishable at γ = 1e-5 and 1e-4 in all regimes. At γ = 1e-3, A–S is lower by 29 / 82 / 10 USDT (low / medium / high). P&L std is 1.6–9× lower for A–S (e.g. medium, γ = 1e-4: 195 vs 1,251 USDT/h; std of final inventory 5.0 vs 19.2 BTC). This is the paper's pattern, at real scale.
- Fees decide profitability: A–S captures 1.65 / 2.06 / 2.57 USDT per fill (low / medium / high, ≈ 1/k whatever γ, because fills land mostly on the side skewed towards the mid), while the fee is ~2.7 USDT at 1 bp and ~5.5 at 2 bp (S ≈ 27,500). Every configuration loses money at 1 and 2 bp (e.g. medium, γ = 1e-4: −262 at 1 bp, −1,306 at 2 bp per hour). All are profitable at 0 bp and with the −0.5 bp rebate (+782 and +1,304).
- With fees, A–S at γ = 1e-3 does worse than naive because it trades more (219 vs 125 fills/h in medium). Its spread narrows towards T and its skew keeps one side close to the mid, while the naive quote stays at the wide average spread.
- Reality check: A–S's optimal spread is 33–165× the real 1-tick spread (3.3–16.5 USDT vs 0.1). The fill-intensity curve is flat (k ≈ 0.4–0.6 per USDT: moving one more USDT away loses only ~40% of fills), so the formula, which assumes we are the only market maker, prices like a monopolist. The real book is competitive: many makers quote at the touch, priority goes by queue position, and the top makers are paid rebates and are profitable at 1 tick because of the rebate and the 1.9/s flow at the touch, not the spread. A–S has no competitors, no queue and no fee term. A fee-aware optimum per side is roughly 1/k + fee·S ≈ 7.5 USDT at 2 bp, wider still.

### Step 1 pre-registration: choice of the fill-intensity model (written and committed before any test-day score is computed)
- 2026-11-26 — **Data.** λ(δ) uses the stage 3 lot rule: with a 0.01 BTC lot, a 1 s window counts as an ask fill at δ only if the buyer-initiated volume printed at prices ≥ mid₀ + δ within the window is ≥ 0.01 BTC (the bid side is the mirror image with seller-initiated volume at ≤ mid₀ − δ). mid₀ = the mid in force when the window opens. Δt = 1 s, ask and bid pooled. Only the 10 calibration days are used for fitting.
- **Candidate (a), relative units.** δ in bps of mid₀ (δ_bps = 10⁴·δ/mid₀), σ in bps. Touch curve on a 0.01 bp grid. Fixed A_rel (/s) and k_rel (/bp) fitted by OLS of ln λ on δ_bps over δ_bps ∈ [0.03, 1.80] (≈ the stage 2 range of 0.1–5 USDT at the calibration price of ~27.8k, starting beyond the touch at every price level), pooled over calibration days, dropping points with < 30 hits.
- **Candidate (b), σ-conditional.** Trailing σ̂ = EWMA (pandas `ewm(span=30)`, i.e. weight α = 2/31 per minute) of squared 60 s mid changes on the UTC-minute grid, divided by 60 s, using only changes that ended at or before the window opens. Windows in the first 30 minutes of a day have no σ̂ and are excluded (no fill-in). The calibration windows (all 10 days pooled) are split into 10 deciles of σ̂; in each decile A and k are fitted in USDT over δ ∈ [0.1, 5.0] (the stage 2 range); then A = a·σ̂^b and k = c·σ̂^d by log-log OLS on the decile medians of σ̂.
- **Score.** For each test day: evaluation windows = windows opening at or after 00:30 UTC with a valid mid₀ and σ̂ (the same windows for both models). For δ on the USDT tick grid {0.1, 0.2, …, 5.0}: p_obs(δ) = share of (window, side) pairs with a lot fill at δ; p_pred(δ) = the mean over the same windows of 1 − exp(−λ_model(δ)·Δt), where λ_model uses only information available when the window opens ((a): A_rel·exp(−k_rel·10⁴δ/mid₀); (b): a·σ̂^b·exp(−c·σ̂^d·δ)). Both are converted to intensities with λ = −ln(1 − p)/Δt. score_day = the mean over δ of |ln(λ_pred/λ_obs)|; δ points with < 30 observed hits on that day are dropped and counted. **Model score = the mean of score_day over the 10 test days** (95% bootstrap CI over days).
- **Rule (fixed now).** Pass mark: score ≤ 0.4 (about a factor of 1.5). Choose (a) if it passes; otherwise (b) if it passes; otherwise fall back to the paper parameters and say so. Both scores are reported whatever happens. For reference only (not part of the rule), the stage 2 pooled-USDT fixed A, k is scored the same way.
- **Use in the simulator.** If (a): regime σ_60s in bps and k_rel are converted to USDT at the regime's price level S₀ (σ = σ_bps·S₀/10⁴, k = k_rel·10⁴/S₀, A = A_rel). If (b): A and k are evaluated at the regime's σ_60s.
